# Family of the non-lucrative resident

> Consolidated BOE text is informative; the official gazette publication is the only legally binding source.

Generated from `data/requirements/family-non-lucrative.json`. RD 1155/2024 art. 61.3.

### Who counts as family for the non-lucrative residence

For this authorisation, ‘family member’ means: (a) the spouse, registered partner, or stable partner of the applicant — a stable partner is a lasting bond; in any event a bond is understood to exist if at least one continuous year of marital cohabitation is proved, unless they have common descendants, in which case proof of stable cohabitation suffices; (b) unmarried minor children, or children who have not formed their own family unit, of the applicant or of the spouse / registered partner / stable partner; (c) adult children of the applicant or of that partner who have a disability requiring support, or who are not objectively able to provide for their own needs because of their health. Dependent ascendants are not in this article’s list — do not import the Ley 14/2013 family circle into this residence.

**In the file:** Marriage, registered-partnership or stable-partnership evidence (one year of cohabitation, or common children plus stable cohabitation); birth certificates; for adult children, disability/health evidence of inability to self-provide.

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 61.3

### Family economic formula: +100% IPREM per family member

In addition to the holder’s 400% IPREM monthly, each family member at the holder’s charge requires a further monthly 100% of IPREM, or its legal equivalent in foreign currency. The global amount is that monthly sum multiplied by the validity sought. Formula only — the current IPREM index is not the article.

**Formula:** `each family member = 100% × IPREM (monthly) × months of validity, added to holder 400% × IPREM × months`

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 62.1.b; BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 62.2

### Insurance must cover the family members who file

Art. 61.2.b (sickness insurance) is a specific requirement of the initial authorisation. The ministry sheet treats the authorisation as one that foreigners and their family members request from the country of origin. Cover each person named in the file.

**In the file:** Insurance certificate listing every family member in the file, with an insurer authorised in Spain.

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 61.2.b; process:nlv-hoja-6

### Adult family members: five-year criminal-record certificates

Art. 38.e applies to visa applicants of criminal-liability age: no criminal record in countries of residence during the last five years. Each adult family member files their own certificates.

**In the file:** Five-year criminal-record certificates for each adult family member, apostilled/legalised and translated.

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 38.e

### How the family file is attached

Art. 61.1 includes family members in the same residence situation. The ministry sheet: the authorisation is requested by foreigners and their family members from the country of origin. Each family member presents EX-01 (or the consular visa form the post currently requires), passport, insurance, criminal record if adult, medical certificate, and the civil-status evidence that places them inside art. 61.3. Means are proved once at household level under art. 62.1–2.

**In the file:** A labelled family annex behind the holder: each person’s EX-01/visa form, passport copy, civil-status document, medical certificate, insurance, and (adults) criminal record.

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 61.1; BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 61.3; process:nlv-hoja-6

### Family members are also without labour or professional activity

The situation defined in art. 61.1 is residence without labour or professional activities, for the foreigner and their family members. Do not assume a family TIE on this route authorises work.

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 61.1

### Renewal: compulsory-age minors must have been in school

Renewal requires that compulsory-school-age minors at the holder’s charge were schooled during their stay in Spain. If schooling is not proved with the application, extranjería notifies the education authorities and warns the applicant in writing that without schooling and the corresponding report within one month, the authorisation will not be renewed.

*Citations:* BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 64.2.d; BOE-A-2024-24099 art. 64.4

## Non-lucrative — family annex

Each family member files from origin with the holder. Family circle is art. 61.3, not Ley 14/2013.

1. EX-01 / visa form per family member
2. Civil-status evidence inside art. 61.3
3. Household means: +100% IPREM per member × months
4. Insurance naming each family member
5. Adults: 5-year criminal-record certificates
6. IHR 2005 medical certificate per person
