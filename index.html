Ley 14/2013 · arts. 74 bis–quinquies

Digital nomad

Remote work for companies outside Spain. A consular visa if you are abroad, up to one year. A UGE-CE residence if you are already lawfully in Spain, up to three years.

RD 1155/2024 · arts. 61–64

Non-lucrative

Live in Spain without working. Filed from your country of origin. Means are 400% of IPREM a month, not a frozen euro number. Then the TIE after you enter.
