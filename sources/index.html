Source of truth

The only records this desk is allowed to speak from.

Consolidated BOE text is informative. The gazette publication is the binding one. Blogs and old consular PDFs are not authority here.

  • BOE-A-2013-10074 — Ley 14/2013, the international-telework residence, articles 74 bis to 74 quinquies.
  • BOE-A-2022-22128 — Ley 28/2022, the amendment that created the telework chapter.
  • BOE-A-2024-24099 — RD 1155/2024, non-lucrative temporary residence, articles 61 to 64.
  • BOE-A-2026-3815 — the SMI index used in the telework formula. An index, not the visa.
  • IPREM — the index in the non-lucrative formula. Published with the budget law, not inside the visa article.

Official procedure pages that may supply a fee, a form name or a filing channel: UGE-CE, Plataforma ONE, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and the live 790 pages. A fee amount is read on the day you pay.
