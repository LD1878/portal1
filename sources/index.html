Source of truth

The law, from the gazette. Nothing else.

Every sentence on this desk is taken from the Boletín Oficial del Estado or from a page published by the ministry that runs the procedure. A blog, a forum, a Facebook group, and an old consular PDF are not sources. They are not read, and they are not quoted.

Watched

Every weekday

A watcher reads the official records each weekday. When the gazette moves, the record here moves with it. The English is reviewed before it changes. Nothing is rewritten in silence.

Two sources

Shown, not picked

Where two official texts disagree, the page shows both and does not choose. The monthly reading of the minimum wage is one of those. A fee is read on the day you pay it, never copied from an old PDF.

Repealed

Not published

Text that is no longer in force is not shown. Consolidated text on the BOE site is informative. The gazette publication is the one that binds.

What this desk is allowed to speak from

BOE-A-2013-10074 — Ley 14/2013, international telework, articles 74 bis to 74 quinquies, and article 62.

BOE-A-2022-21739 — Ley 28/2022, the amendment that created that chapter.

BOE-A-2024-24099 — RD 1155/2024, non-lucrative residence, articles 61 to 64.

BOE-A-2000-544 — Ley Orgánica 4/2000, the law those articles regulate.

BOE-A-2026-3815 — the minimum wage for 2026. An index, not the visa.

BOE-A-2022-22128 — the budget law that sets IPREM. An index, not the visa.

The joint instruction of the two ministries, the UGE digital-nomad FAQ, and the ministry sheets for the forms and the fees are official procedure pages. They supply the form number, the fee code, and the deadline. They do not amend the statute.
