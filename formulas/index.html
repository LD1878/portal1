Money is a formula

A percentage of an index. Not a frozen euro number.

Non-lucrative

400% of monthly IPREM for you, plus 100% for each family member, times the months you apply for. At €600 IPREM, a holder alone illustrates at €2,400 a month. When the index moves, the illustration moves. The article does not.

Digital nomad

200% of SMI for you, 75% for the first family member, 25% for each person after. Gazette monthly SMI is €1,221, so a holder illustrates at €2,442. A second official reading divides the annual floor by twelve. Both exist because the sources disagree.
