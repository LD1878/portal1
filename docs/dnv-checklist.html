Digital nomad file checklist

International telework, Ley 14/2013. Remote work for companies outside Spain. Not for EU, EEA or Swiss nationals. Tick the file in this order.

Two doors. Outside Spain: consular visa, maximum 1 year. Already lawfully in Spain: UGE-CE residence, maximum 3 years. You cannot file the UGE residence from abroad.

Holder packet

  1. 01 Form. National visa form if you are at a consulate. Signed MI-T if you file with UGE-CE.
  2. 02 Fee. Consular visa-fee receipt, or tasa 038 paid with the NRC kept.
  3. 03 Passport. Colour copy of every page. Bring the original to the consulate.
  4. 04 Company. Register certificate: at least 1 year of real, continuous activity.
  5. 05 Work link. Employed: 3 months with that employer, plus a remote-work letter (role, pay, terms). Professional: 3 months of contracts or invoices, plus the remote terms.
  6. 06 Remote only. Written proof the work is done only by computer or telecoms.
  7. 07 Qualification. Degree or recognised school, or 3 years in a similar role.
  8. 08 Means. 200% of monthly SMI for the holder. Use the formula, not a frozen euro amount.
  9. 09 Criminal record. Certificates for countries of residence in the last 2 years, plus a 5-year sworn declaration. UGE FAQ: this can be waived after more than 6 months of Spanish stay. Treat that as a conflict, not as one settled rule.
  10. 10 Insurance. Public cover, or private full cover from an insurer authorised in Spain. Not travel insurance.
  11. 11 Social Security. Coverage certificate, or a commitment to register in Spain before the work starts.
  12. 12 Foreign papers. Apostille or legalisation, then a sworn Spanish translation, filed behind each document.

Family annex

One form per person (visa form, or MI-F at UGE). Civil-status proof. Insurance in their name. Adults: the same criminal-record packet. Means on top of the holder: 75% of monthly SMI for the first family member, then 25% of monthly SMI for each person after that.

After you file

Consular visa: up to 1 year. Ask for the 3-year residence in the 60 days before it ends. UGE-CE: up to 3 years, decision in 20 working days, silence is positive. Get a TIE if the stay is longer than 6 months.

Not legal advice. English operating note from official BOE and procedure records only. Check the competent consulate or UGE-CE page before you file. Money is a formula (SMI), not a euro figure in the statute. jurospain.com
