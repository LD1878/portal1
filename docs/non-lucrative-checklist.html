Non-lucrative file checklist

Temporary residence with no work, RD 1155/2024 arts. 61 to 64. Not for EU, EEA or Swiss nationals. Tick the file in this order.

One door. File at the competent Spanish consulate. The visa application is the authorisation. Initial grant: 1 year. There is no initial UGE-CE path for this residence.

Holder packet

  1. 01 Form. EX-01, completed and signed.
  2. 02 Passport. Copy with at least 1 year of validity left. Bring the original.
  3. 03 Criminal record. Certificates covering the last 5 years in your country of origin and any other country where you lived.
  4. 04 Means. 400% of monthly IPREM for the holder, times the months you ask for. Use the formula, not a frozen euro amount.
  5. 05 Proof of means. Foreign bank letter: legal name, address, account IDs, opening date, 31 December balance, and last-year average.
  6. 06 Insurance. Sickness cover with an insurer authorised in Spain.
  7. 07 Medical certificate. No disease with serious public-health impact under the International Health Regulations 2005.
  8. 08 Fees. Modelo 790 code 052, heading 2.1.1, plus the consular visa fee.
  9. 09 Foreign papers. Apostille or legalisation, then a sworn Spanish translation, filed behind each document.

Family annex

Each person files from origin with you. Their own EX-01 or visa form. Civil-status proof. Medical certificate. Insurance in their name. Adults: 5-year criminal records. Means on top of the holder: 100% of monthly IPREM per family member, times the months.

After you file

Decision: 1 month from the consulate's report to extranjeria. Silence is negative. Collect the visa within 1 month. Enter while it is valid (maximum 3 months). TIE within 1 month of entry. Renewal is 2 years and needs more than 183 days of real residence in the year. File in the 2 months before expiry.
